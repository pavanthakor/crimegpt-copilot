## Complaint (English)

I am the proprietor of Shreeji Trading Co., Ashram Road, Ellisbridge. The accused, Kirit M.
(age about 34), was employed as my accounts assistant and was entrusted with collecting cash
payments from customers and depositing them into the firm's bank account. On reconciling the
accounts for the quarter April to June 2026, I found that on several dates the accused collected
cash totalling approximately Rs. 3,40,000 from customers, issued receipts, but did not deposit
the amounts into the firm's account and instead dishonestly misappropriated the money for his
own use. When questioned, the accused admitted receiving the payments but failed to account for
them and has since stopped attending work. The customer receipt book and the firm's ledger
support the shortfall. I request that legal action be taken and the amount recovered.
