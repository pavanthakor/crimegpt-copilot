## Complaint (English)

On 12/07/2026, between about 01:00 and 03:00 hrs, while my family and I were asleep at home,
an unknown person broke the iron grille of the rear window of my house and entered. From the
cupboard in the living room he stole two gold bangles, one mangalsutra and cash of Rs. 18,000,
and fled. In the morning I found the cupboard open and the belongings scattered. The society
watchman states that during the night he saw an unknown person running away along the rear lane.
